# Guided intake

Use this wording on the first incomplete invocation. Begin with the welcome and support line exactly as written. Prefill known answers and omit resolved requests. Keep one concrete example after every unanswered item; do not remove or collapse the examples when presenting the form.

> Welcome to Outbound OS, by Dean Hunt. If you hit any major snags, feel free to reach out to Dean on Slack, or at dean@tiki.com - all feedback, bug reports, and suggestions are welcomed.
>
> Please provide a list or spreadsheet of target accounts. You can upload a CSV, XLSX, or TSV file, paste a list, or share a connected Google Sheet. Each account needs at least a name or website.
>
> Please also describe:
>
> 1. **How the accounts were selected** — for example, a CRM export, conference list, partner directory, hand-built research list, or lookalikes of your best customers.
> 2. **Target industries or account types** — for example, tourism boards, hotel groups, software platforms, retailers, agencies, or marketplaces.
> 3. **Markets or geographies** — for example, UK, DACH, North America, or EMEA; clarify whether this means headquarters, operations, or customers served.
> 4. **Relevant size measures** — for example, annual revenue, employee count, number of locations or properties, website traffic, or audience size.
> 5. **Must-have characteristics and exclusions** — for example, must operate multiple locations or use a particular technology; exclude existing customers, agencies, or pure-content sites.
> 6. **Useful thresholds** — for example, at least £5m revenue, 50 employees, 10 locations, or 100,000 monthly visits.
> 7. **Your name, organization, and sender’s name if different** — for example, “I’m Alex Smith at Example Company, and I’ll be the sender.”
> 8. **What you’re offering** — for example, an analytics platform, commercial partnership, consultancy service, sponsorship, or data product.
> 9. **Campaign goal and desired outcome** — for example, book a discovery call, confirm partnership interest, arrange a product demo, or identify the right decision-maker.
> 10. **Intended channel** — for example, Gmail draft, Outlook draft, or a manual form/export route.
> 11. **Campaign name** — for example, “UK Hotel Partnerships — Q4 2026.”

If a reusable team profile exists, show its user name, organization, sender, default offer, and channels and ask whether this campaign overrides them. Never display or store credentials.

Once the user supplies or confirms their name, address them by that name in every subsequent user-facing response in this workflow. Use it naturally at least once per response; do not repeat it mechanically in every paragraph.

Required before final confirmation:

- a usable account source;
- campaign goal and desired outcome;
- offer or reason for outreach;
- user name, organization, and sender identity, either supplied or selected from a profile;
- explicit targeting rules, even when one or more rule groups are empty;
- intended channels;
- a campaign name.
